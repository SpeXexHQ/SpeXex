OTC Atomic Swap

Trade ROD for LTC, DOGE, and other supported chains directly from your browser using trustless atomic swaps. No intermediary, no custodian — ECDSA adaptor signatures ensure both parties settle or both refund. Orders are published on-chain via the ROD name database and coordinated over Nostr relays.

Open OTC Swap

Trustless

Adaptor signatures and timelocked refunds guarantee that either the swap completes for both sides, or both sides can reclaim their coins.

On-chain Orderbook

Orders are anchored in the ROD name database — a public, tamper-proof order book that anyone can verify. No off-chain server decides what gets listed.

Multi-chain

ROD, LTC, and DOGE are currently swap-certified and any two can be paired in either direction. BTC, BCH, and DGB currently have wallet-level support only. More chains are being added through the same reviewed registry.

Access Your ROD Wallet local, browser-based wallet

Your wallet is generated locally from your email and passphrase. Switch networks with the Coins menu. Nothing is uploaded, stored, or recoverable by this app.

Open wallet

Enter the same email and passphrase every time to derive the same wallet in this browser. Address format follows the active coin (ROD). A different email or passphrase will generate a different wallet.

Compatibility notice: This wallet keeps the slightly less secure encryption for compatibility, which makes using a really strong, unique passphrase much more important. Weak or reused credentials put your funds at risk.

Use a phrase you have not used anywhere else. This is not stored or recoverable.
